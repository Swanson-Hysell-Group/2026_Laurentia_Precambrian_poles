# Francois ca. 1476 Ma paleomagnetic pole

## Geologic Context

- Terrane: Laurentia.
- Compiled unit/pole label: St.Francois Mountains Acidic Rocks.

## Paleomagnetic And Rock Magnetic Data

- B = 18 sites, N = 103 samples; mean direction DEC/INC = 233.4°/36.9°; K = 27.0.
- Field/statistical tests noted in compilation: C*+,F+,G+.
- Rock-magnetic interpretation: expand with mineral carriers/coercivity/unblocking evidence from source studies.

## Age Constraints

- Current bracket: nominal age 1466 Ma, lomagage 1448.0 Ma, himagage 1484.0 Ma.
- Compilation method note: Previously cited 1476$\pm$16 Ma from Van Schmus et al. 1993 by Meert and Stuckey, 2002. In 2021 there is a USGS study that compiled previous dates from the St. Francois Mountains, and dated a lot more units with zircon and titanite U-Pb. They generally found two episodes of magmatism, one at 1.48-1.45 Ga and another at 1.33-1.28 Ga, with the former episode being more voluminous and widespread. The 1.48-1.45 Ga rocks include the dolerites sampled for pmag. A reasonable range and middle point age for this unit paleomagnetic data is 1448, 1484, and 1466 Ma, taking the age range of the in-situ dates from the many units dated by the new study.
