## Checks performed against the source publications

* **VGPs.** All 60 VGPs of the source compilation were recomputed from the site mean
  directions and coordinates with `pmag.dia_vgp`. Agreement is exact to 0.003° in VGP
  latitude and 0.02° in VGP longitude, the residual reflecting rounding in the DuBois
  (1962) site means recomputed from sample-level data. Reverse-polarity VGPs are
  reported as the antipodal north pole.
* **Pesonen (1979).** The compilation's ten Pesonen entries (R40–R49) are the "Logan
  reversed sills and adjacent baked contact rocks" of that paper. Their Fisher mean
  reproduces the published D = 112.6°, I = −70.3°, K = 55, N = 10 (recomputed:
  D = 112.7°, I = −70.3°, K = 54.7). Note that the published α95 = 4.6° is inconsistent
  with K = 55 and N = 10, which give α95 = 6.6°. Site-level values for R40–R49 are not
  tabulated in the 1979 journal paper itself, which refers to Pesonen (1978). The
  Geological Survey of Finland report version (Report 1942, Q 20/27.2/79/1) carries the
  same tables plus the sampling-site maps; its Figure 1 is the only published source for
  the positions of the ten sill sites and confirms the compilation's digitized
  coordinates to better than ~2 km for every site except the R47/R49 pair.
* **Middleton et al. (2004).** All 15 entries were matched to that paper's Table 1 by
  site number, direction, k, α95 and n. Table 1 lists 15 rows for 9 sampling localities,
  the repeats being thermal (T), low-temperature (L) and combined (L & T) treatments of
  the same specimens.
* **Borradaile & Middleton (2006).** The entries `SI`, `IS` and `PL_*` were matched to
  that paper's Table 2 (Seagull Intrusion, Inspiration Sills, and the four Pillar Lake
  Lava treatments). Note that `n` in Table 2 counts specimens, not sites.
* **DuBois (1962).** The site groupings (`N1m`, `N9-11`, `N12-15`, …) correspond to the
  sample-level directions of that paper's Table XIII, with the wildly discordant samples
  (e.g. N9, N18) omitted from the site means.

## Same-sill correlations found in the papers

Applied in `ADDITIONAL_MERGES`:

* **DuBois `N27-29` into Red Rock North.** DuBois Table XIII places N27, N28 and N29 at
  one cliff above the CPR and CNR tracks at Red Rock — N27/N28 "from sediment immediately
  below diabase sill", N29 "from lower part of the sill". The compilation splits them into
  `N29 BCT` (the baked sediment) and `N27-29` (the sill), but a sill and the sediment it
  baked record one cooling event.
* **Robertson & Fahrig `S7` into Mt Mackay.** S7 plots 0.33 km from DuBois's Mount Mackay
  site, the lower Mount McKay sill at Fort William dated by Bleeker et al. (2020) at
  1106.3 ± 2.0 Ma. DuBois's own directions there are badly scattered (his Table XV; he
  invoked a superimposed random component and used great-circle fits); S7 (k = 92,
  α95 = 10°) is much the better determination of the same sill.

**Refuted:** merging Pesonen `R49` into Kama Hill (`R47` + Robertson & Fahrig `S14`). R47
and R49 carry identical coordinates and identical notes in the source compilation, which
suggested one sill. The Geological Survey of Finland report version of Pesonen (1979)
(Report 1942, Q 20/27.2/79/1) settles it: the 270 hand samples came "from 18 reversed and
22 normal dikes and from **10 reversed sills**", and its Figure 1 plots R47 and R49 as two
separate boxed sill sites on the north shore of Nipigon Bay, R49 slightly north of R47.
Sites R40–R49 are ten distinct sills by Pesonen's own count.

Listed in `CANDIDATE_MERGES` but **not** applied, because the publications do not resolve
them — Robertson & Fahrig (1971) describe none of their sites, so those positions are
digitized from that paper's Figure 1:

* Robertson & Fahrig `S8` with DuBois `N19-21` — 3.5 km apart on the Highway 11 scarp
  south of Orient Bay.
* DuBois `N16-18` with `N9-11` — DuBois locates N16 "2 miles southwest of Ozone Station"
  and N9–N11 "just south of Ozone Station".

Neither moves the pole by more than 0.5°.

### The limit on "one VGP per sill"

The Logan sills are laterally extensive sheets that transgress from one bedding plane to
another (Robertson & Fahrig, 1971), so exposures tens of kilometres apart can belong to
one cooling unit. The source compilation already merges Pesonen `R45` into the Terry Fox
unit across ~27 km on the basis of the Ontario geological map. Applied consistently, that
kind of map-based correlation would probably reduce the unit count further. **N = 37 is an
upper bound on the number of independent cooling units**, not a verified count.

## Other findings from the source publications

* **DuBois `P8m` is not a usable determination.** Table XIII: "From what appears to be
  diabase sill at Jarvis Bay". Its two samples give 326.0/−63.0 and 121.5/−68.5, i.e.
  α95 = 138°, and DuBois himself was unsure the body is a sill. Recommend excluding it.
* **DuBois `N31m` is a dyke, not a sill.** Table XIII: "N31PA and N31PB from dyke that
  cuts Animikie and Sibley sediments and Sleeping Giant sill; other samples from sill" —
  the latter being `N32-34`. As a crosscutting body it is younger than the sill it cuts.
* **DuBois `SN16`–`SN29` are absent from the compilation** — the Pardee Township body
  (SN16–SN25) and the Horne Falls diabase on the Pigeon River (SN26–SN29). Their
  sample directions are scattered, so DuBois may have set them aside, but the omission
  should be deliberate rather than accidental.
* **Robertson & Fahrig `S15` has no direction** and appears only as a baked-contact site,
  so their site list in the compilation (12 accepted + S4 and S11 excluded) is complete.
* **Pesonen's site means already combine sill and baked contact rock.** His Figure 1
  caption lists baked samples at R40, R41, R42, R43, R44, R46, R47 and R48, and his N = 10
  mean is of "Logan reversed sills and adjacent baked contact rocks (combined)", so there
  is no double counting within his sites.
* **This is a sills-only pole.** Pesonen's reversed Thunder Bay *dikes* (R22–R38, his
  Table 6) are a separate result with its own pole at 48.0°N / 212.2°E and are absent
  from the compilation — whereas the Luleå Working Group grand mean that this entry
  replaces did include them. Pesonen notes the dikes cut the sills, so they are younger.
* **Pesonen `R45` is at Pass Lake, not at Terry Fox.** His Figure 1 places R45 at the neck
  of the Sibley Peninsula, ~27 km northeast of the Current River / Terry Fox exposure. The
  source compilation's merge of R45 into the Terry Fox unit therefore asserts a single
  sill sheet continuous over that distance, traced from the Ontario geological map. This
  is plausible for these sills but should be confirmed.
* **Pesonen `R40` is at Rossport**, the easternmost site in the compilation, adjacent to
  the Osler Volcanics on his Figure 1 — which is what the compilation's `Area` label
  "Osler" refers to. It is a reversed sill, not an Osler lava.
* **Middleton `M12` (Fox Mountain) is described in that paper as the "Fox Mountain
  diabase dike"**, not a sill.

## Open questions for the compiler

1. `result_quality` in the source compilation is not used here. Its `g`/`b` assignment
   does not track n, k or α95 in an obvious way (e.g. Middleton `M1`, k = 50.3, is `b`
   while `M11`, k = 10.2, is `g`) and needs clarification.
2. The `site.1` column of the source spreadsheet is a copy of `site` shifted by one row
   from row 18 onward and was dropped. The `site` column is the one consistent with the
   `Reference` column.
3. Some `Area` labels appear misplaced — DuBois `SN5-15`, at Current River in Thunder
   Bay, is labelled "Pigeon". (`R40` labelled "Osler" is correct: Pesonen's Figure 1 puts
   it at Rossport adjacent to the Osler Volcanics.) Regions here are the two-way grouping
   "Nipigon Embayment" / "Thunder Bay-Logan"; the finer `Area` assignment should be
   checked against the Ontario geological map before any finer subdivision is used.
4. The Seagull Intrusion is dated 1112 ± 2.4 Ma (Hart & Whaley, 2005) in Borradaile &
   Middleton (2006), but the source compilation notes 1107.8 ± 1.4 Ma. Which date is
   intended?
5. Cross-study sill correlations marked with a query in the source compilation
   ("Terry Fox?" for Pesonen `R45`, "Channel Island sill?" for `R40`) are treated as
   correlations here; `R45` is therefore merged into the Terry Fox unit, over a 27 km
   separation. Please confirm against the Ontario geological map.
6. Pesonen `R47` and `R49` should be given distinct coordinates — Figure 1 of the
   Geological Survey of Finland report version places R49 slightly north of R47.
