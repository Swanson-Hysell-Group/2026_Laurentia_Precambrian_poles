The pole is the Fisher mean of the virtual geomagnetic poles of the three dykes (2, 4 and 6) that share a southeast-and-down characteristic direction interpreted by the authors as primary; each dyke is treated as one cooling unit and its VGP is computed from the dyke-mean direction. It is reported at the southern-hemisphere pole as in the paper. Three other dykes (1, 3 and 5) carry mutually different 'anomalous' directions that the authors interpret as disturbed paleomagnetic signatures and exclude. The remanence is carried by magnetite (Curie point 570 C; multidomain behaviour in the Lowrie-Fuller test) and was isolated mostly by alternating-field demagnetization, with thermal demagnetization of some samples, principal-component analysis and remagnetization circles. A baked-contact test on dyke 2 (one host-gabbro sample each at the contact, in a hybrid zone at 8.5 m and at 13 m) is interpreted by the authors as showing the dyke remanence to be primary. Site coordinates are not given in the paper and were read from its maps. U-Pb baddeleyite-zircon dating of dyke 4 (Kamo & Gower, 1994) ties the primary direction to the ca. 615 Ma emplacement age of the swarm determined on dyke 1 (Kamo et al., 1989); K-Ar ages on dykes 2 and 4 are interpreted as post-emplacement cooling ages.
