# Laurentia paleolatitude through time

The poles in this compilation imply a paleolatitude for any locality on Laurentia through time. The figures below track the paleolatitude of Duluth, Minnesota (46.79°N, 92.10°W) — on the southern margin of the Superior province — implied by each pole, following the top panel of Figure 4.5 of [Swanson-Hysell (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-818533-9.00009-6). Markers are colored and shaped by terrane and reliability grade; vertical bars are A{sub}`95` and horizontal bars are the age uncertainty. Poles uncertain by more than 50 Myr are omitted.

The compilation spans 1800 Ma to the present. Precambrian poles are the site-level recreations from the 2022 Kringerdalen and 2026 Iloranta workshops; Phanerozoic poles are the Laurentia subset of the Torsvik et al. (2012) compilation. Bands mark the durations of Nuna (1820–1380 Ma), Rodinia (1060–718 Ma), and Pangea (320–175 Ma).
