# Laurentia paleolatitude through time

The paleomagnetic poles in this compilation imply a paleolatitude for any locality on Laurentia through time. The figures below track the paleolatitude of Duluth, Minnesota (46.79°N, 92.10°W) — on the southern margin of the Superior province — implied by each pole, following the top panel of Figure 4.5 of [Swanson-Hysell (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-818533-9.00009-6).

In each figure the upper panel is the compilation as of the 2017 Nordic workshop ([Evans et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-818533-9.00007-2)) and the lower panel is the updated compilation that incorporates the 2022 Kringerdalen and 2026 Iloranta workshops. Markers are colored and shaped by terrane and reliability grade; vertical bars are A95 and horizontal bars are the age uncertainty (poles uncertain by more than 50 Myr are omitted). Transparent bands mark the Grenvillian Orogeny (Rodinia assembly), the stable supercontinent interior, and Rodinia break-up.
